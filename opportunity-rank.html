Model V1 · formulas, guardrails and limitations

The chat proposed EBR's 50/35/15 structure but did not fully define scoring functions or the original Buying Strength formula. This page uses an explicit V1 implementation. Its thresholds and 60/40 blend are hypotheses, not backtested or calibrated probabilities. A score of 80 does not mean an 80% chance of success. No trade orders are placed.

EBR = clamp(Core + max(Continuation, Launch) + Execution − Extension penalty, 0, 100). Core contributes 50 points, each branch up to 35, and execution up to 15. Click a scored ticker for every factor’s exact scale. Extension above EMA20: ≤5% = 0 penalty; >5–10% = 2; >10–15% = 5; >15% = 10.

Buying Strength has ten equally weighted 10-point factors: 21/63/126-session returns; price vs SMA20/50/100; proximity to the 63-session closing high; CMF20; RVOL20; and signed-volume imbalance. This is a documented proxy for the chat’s original ranking, not a reproduction of unavailable original weights. Linear scales and clamping are shown in the breakdown.

Guardrails: closing price <$50 and 20-day average dollar volume ≥$100,000. Stocks outside these guardrails can show component scores but receive no final score or rank. These are the chat's research constraints, not a guarantee of tradability. Rank is recomputed within the selected universe; search and data-state filters preserve that universe’s rank numbers. Full-precision scores determine order, with ticker order resolving ties.

Readiness: READY requires 0<ROC7<20%, 0<normalized VPCI<1.5, five-session maximum VMI≥1.00, close>EMA20, plus either rising VPCI versus five sessions ago with RVOL≥0.8, or negative ROC in one of the previous three sessions with ATR%≥4. EARLY means at least three of the four core conditions pass but the full trigger does not. Otherwise NOT READY. Branch identifies whichever subscore is higher; a tie uses continuation. A high score does not override readiness.

Indicators: ROC7 is close/close seven sessions ago −1. EMA20 starts at the first supplied close. RSI14 and ATR14 use Wilder smoothing. MFI14 uses typical-price money flows; VMI=1+(MFI−RSI)/100. Normalized VPCI matches the repository variant: (VWMA10−SMA10) × (VWMA50/SMA50) × (mean volume10/mean volume50) ÷ SMA50. CMF20 is volume-weighted close location within each bar (zero for zero-range bars). RVOL divides today's volume by the previous 20 sessions’ mean. Dollar volume averages close×volume over the latest 20 observations. Signed-volume imbalance is (up-session volume−down-session volume)/total volume over 20 sessions. The execution volatility factor is a proxy, not a quoted bid/ask spread.

Data: at least 130 complete, date-aligned OHLCV observations; no duplicate dates, invalid bars, or gaps over seven calendar days. The latest bar must be no more than seven days old and not in the future. Unavailable data never earns zero points—it produces an unranked data-pending row. Prices are feed observations, not verified live quotes. A ticker present in multiple feeds uses the newest complete, usable history; otherwise the most recent attempted source. Source coverage is listed above.

Historical performance and in-sample examples do not establish predictive value. This V1 overlaps price/volume factors and needs chronological, out-of-sample testing with point-in-time universes, transaction costs and capital constraints. It is a research view, not a personalised buy/sell recommendation. Backtest overfitting research ↗
