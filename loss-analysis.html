How to read this page

A losing position has current market value below its cost basis. Unrealized P&L = (sheet price − average cost) × units. Return = unrealized P&L ÷ cost basis. Gain to break even = (average cost ÷ sheet price − 1) × 100; it assumes the share count and cost basis stay unchanged. At a zero price, the required percentage gain is undefined.

Portfolio weight uses the current value of all open holdings, including profitable positions. Share of losses uses only the combined dollar losses on this page. Summary cards and concentration bars always cover all classified losing positions; table totals follow your filters. Missing quotes or costs are listed below and excluded from loss classification. Portfolio weights are unavailable if any position value is unknown.

These figures exclude realized trades, cash, fees, dividends and taxes. Download time is shown above; the feed does not supply quote timestamps. A price-only loss does not explain a company's prospects or determine whether to buy or sell.
